# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participation must be professional, technically honest, and welcoming.
Be respectful across differences in experience, identity, language, and
background. Critique code and evidence, not people.

Acceptable participation includes giving reproducible feedback, acknowledging
uncertainty, correcting mistakes openly, and helping others understand the
system. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, or publishing another
person's private information.

## Technical Integrity

Do not fabricate test results, conceal regressions, misstate implementation
status, or present CaribeOS as an Apple product. Responsible disagreement about
architecture, licensing, security, or evidence is welcome when expressed
constructively.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. For a sensitive report,
contact the repository owner privately through the contact methods available on
their GitHub profile; do not place private details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed in context and handled with as much confidentiality as
the platform permits. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
